# Usability Testing

Usability testing is how we check that what we've designed actually works for the people who'll use it. This skill helps you prepare, run, and synthesize a usability test — from writing the script to summarizing findings.

## When to use this

- You have a prototype or design that needs validation before engineering picks it up
- You want to identify usability issues before they become expensive to fix
- You need structured findings to share with your PM or stakeholders

## What you'll need to do

The agent handles the prep work (script, tasks, questions, findings report), but you'll need to:

1. **Schedule sessions** — recruit 3–5 participants who match your target persona. Coordinate calendars, send invites, and confirm attendance.
2. **Run the sessions** — facilitate the test using the generated script. The agent can't be in the room with you.
3. **Take notes** — capture non-verbal observations (hesitations, wrong clicks, facial expressions) during each session. These don't show up in transcripts and are critical for analysis.
4. **Share raw notes** — paste your session notes back so the agent can synthesize findings.

## Tips for success

- Don't skip the non-verbal notes. Transcripts miss the most important signals.
- Include your PM or a dev as an observer when possible — it builds shared understanding.
- Run your post-test debrief within 24 hours while observations are fresh.
- 5 participants is ideal, but 3 is enough to spot major issues.
