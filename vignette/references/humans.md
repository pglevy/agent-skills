# Vignette

A vignette is a short, cinematic HTML animation that tells a visual story about a product feature. Think of it as a product theater piece — a polished demo you can embed in a presentation, share in chat, or use to get stakeholders excited about what you're building.

## When to use this

- You need to demo a feature that doesn't exist yet (or isn't fully wired up)
- You want to show a workflow end-to-end without clicking through a live prototype
- You're presenting to leadership and want something more compelling than static screenshots

## What you'll need to provide

A brief describing the story you want to tell. The agent will give you a template, but the key pieces are:

1. **Beats** — the sequence of moments in your story (what the camera shows, what happens on screen)
2. **Reference images** — screenshots or mockups showing the UI content for each beat
3. **Narrative** — what captions should say to guide the viewer

> NOTE: When providing image references, you have to paste them into the chat since Kiro cannot "see" image data from file references.

## What you'll get back

A single self-contained HTML file with GSAP-powered animations. Open it in a browser and it plays like a short film of your product in action.

## Tips for a compelling vignette

- Keep it under 60 seconds. Shorter is better.
- Focus on one workflow or feature — don't try to show everything.
- Use real-looking content (names, data, messages) to make it feel authentic.
- Think about pacing: establish context wide, then zoom into the action.
