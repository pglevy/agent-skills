# Quick Mockup

A quick mockup is a low-fidelity HTML representation of a feature or screen you're designing. It's not a polished prototype — it's a conversation starter that helps your team align on direction before investing in detailed design work.

## When to use this

- You have a new feature idea and need to show stakeholders what you're thinking
- You're exploring multiple layout options and want something more tangible than a sketch
- You need to communicate a design direction to engineering early in the process

## What you'll need to provide

- A text description of what you want to mock up (a feature brief, PRD section, or even a few bullet points)
- Optionally, a reference screenshot or image to match

## What you'll get back

One or more standalone HTML pages styled using the Sailwind design tokens. These are meant to be viewed in a browser and shared with your team for feedback — not shipped to production.

## Tips for a good brief

- Be specific about what screens or states you need (e.g., "empty state, populated state, error state")
- Call out any interactions that matter for understanding the flow
- If you have brand or layout constraints, mention them upfront
